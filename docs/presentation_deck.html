Data Modeling

Embedding vs. Referencing

Embedded: Supplier contact and line items embedded within Purchase Orders to eliminate multi-table join overhead during frequent lookups.

Referenced: supplier_id in products and product_id in sales records prevent unbounded document growth and enable independent updates.

Unique SKU Index: Guarantees catalog uniqueness directly at database engine.

Procurement Intelligence

Reorder Point (ROP) & Safety Stock Math

Translating machine learning predictions into precise, automated procurement actions.

Step 1: Lead Time Demand

Demand_LT = d̂ × L

Multiplies expected daily demand (d̂) from ML predictions by supplier turnaround lead time (L in days).

Example: 15 units/day × 6 days lead time = 90 units consumed during shipment.
Step 2: Safety Stock Cushion

SS = Z × √L × σ_d

Buffers against demand variance (σ_d) using statistical service factor Z = 1.65 (95% service level confidence).

Higher sales volatility automatically generates a larger inventory cushion.
Step 3: Reorder Point & Qty

ROP = Demand_LT + SS

Order trigger activates when Current_Stock ≤ ROP. Order quantity calculates: ROP - Current_Stock + Batch_Size.

Aligns with supplier Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) and batch multiples.
